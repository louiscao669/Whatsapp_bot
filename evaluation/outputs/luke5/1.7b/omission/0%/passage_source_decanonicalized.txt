__PERSON_F__ Calls His First Disciples
1 One day as __PERSON_F__ was standing by the Lake of Gennesaret,[a] the people were crowding around him and listening to the word of __MOST_HIGH_A__. 2 He saw at the water’s edge two boats, left there by the fishermen, who were washing their nets. 3 He got into one of the boats, the one belonging to Simon, and asked him to put out a little from shore. Then he sat down and taught the people from the boat.

4 When he had finished speaking, he said to Simon, “Put out into deep water, and let down the nets for a catch.”

1 Simon answered, “Master, we’ve worked hard all night and haven’t caught anything. But because you say so, I will let down the nets.”

6 When they had done so, they caught such a large number of fish that their nets began to break. 7 So they signaled their partners in the other boat to come and help them, and they came and filled both boats so full that they began to sink.

8 When Simon Peter saw this, he fell at __PERSON_F__ knees and said, “Go away from me, __MASTER_A__; I am a sinful man!” 9 For he and all his companions were astonished at the catch of fish they had taken, 10 and so were James and __PERSON_C__, the sons of Zebedee, Simon’s partners.

Then __PERSON_F__ said to Simon, “Don’t be afraid; from now on you will fish for people.” 11 So they pulled their boats up on shore, left everything and followed him.

__PERSON_F__ Heals a Man With Leprosy
12 While __PERSON_F__ was in one of the towns, a man came along who was covered with leprosy.[b] When he saw __PERSON_F__, he fell with his face to the ground and begged him, “__MASTER_A__, if you are willing, you can make me clean.”

13 __PERSON_F__ reached out his hand and touched the man. “I am willing,” he said. “Be clean!” And immediately the leprosy left him.

14 Then __PERSON_F__ ordered him, “Don’t tell anyone, but go, show yourself to the __WORKER_A__ and offer the sacrifices that Moses commanded for your cleansing, as a testimony to them.”

15 Yet the news about him spread all the more, so that crowds of people came to hear him and to be healed of their sicknesses. 16 But __PERSON_F__ often withdrew to lonely places and prayed.

__PERSON_F__ Forgives and Heals a Paralyzed Man
17 One day __PERSON_F__ was teaching, and Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting there. They had come from every village of __REGION_A__ and from Judea and __CITY_A__. And the power of the __MASTER_A__ was with __PERSON_F__ to heal the sick. 18 Some men came carrying a paralyzed man on a mat and tried to take him into the house to lay him before __PERSON_F__. 19 When they could not find a way to do this because of the crowd, they went up on the roof and lowered him on his mat through the tiles into the middle of the crowd, right in front of __PERSON_F__.

20 When __PERSON_F__ saw their faith, he said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.”

21 The Pharisees and the teachers of the law began thinking to themselves, “Who is this fellow who speaks blasphemy? Who can forgive sins but __MOST_HIGH_A__ alone?”

22 __PERSON_F__ knew what they were thinking and asked, “Why are you thinking these things in your hearts? 23 Which is easier: to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Get up and walk’? 24 But I want you to know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins.” So he said to the paralyzed man, “I tell you, get up, take your mat and go home.” 25 Immediately he stood up in front of them, took what he had been lying on and went home praising __MOST_HIGH_A__. 26 Everyone was amazed and gave praise to __MOST_HIGH_A__. They were filled with awe and said, “We have seen remarkable things today.”

__PERSON_F__ Calls Levi and Eats With Sinners
27 After this, __PERSON_F__ went out and saw a tax collector by the name of Levi sitting at his tax booth. “Follow me,” __PERSON_F__ said to him, 28 and Levi got up, left everything and followed him.

29 Then Levi held a great banquet for __PERSON_F__ at his house, and a large crowd of tax collectors and others were eating with them. 30 But the Pharisees and the teachers of the law who belonged to their sect complained to his disciples, “Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”

31 __PERSON_F__ answered them, “It is not the healthy who need a doctor, but the sick. 32 I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”

__PERSON_F__ Questioned About Fasting
33 They said to him, “__PERSON_C__ disciples often fast and pray, and so do the disciples of the Pharisees, but yours go on eating and drinking.”

34 __PERSON_F__ answered, “Can you make the friends of the bridegroom fast while he is with them? 35 But the time will come when the bridegroom will be taken from them; in those days they will fast.”

36 He told them this parable: “No one tears a piece out of a new garment to patch an old one. Otherwise, they will have torn the new garment, and the patch from the new will not match the old. 37 And no one pours new wine into old wineskins. Otherwise, the new wine will burst the skins; the wine will run out and the wineskins will be ruined. 38 No, new wine must be poured into new wineskins. 39 And no one after drinking old wine wants the new, for they say, ‘The old is better.’”
