80 mixed cells (5 defect pairs × doses × 8 chapters). Estimand: does stacking two defects equal the sum of their individual damage? Interaction I = observed drop − (drop_A + drop_B). I > 0 = superadditive (worse than the sum).

Why the raw-accuracy view, not the logit-Δ script output. The single-defect MCQ conditions sit near the ceiling (0.89–0.95 vs baseline 0.93), where the 1PL common-shift Δ-fitter is ill-conditioned — it reports MCQ as "subadditive −5 logits", which inverts the real effect. In accuracy space (immune to the ceiling blow-up) both MCQ and open are cleanly superadditive. Treat additivity_mixed_1.7b's logit pooled value with caution; the accuracy-space interaction above is the trustworthy read.

Implications

  • Absolute thresholds are NOT re-licensed — the opposite. A single-λ additive burden model (deployment §3) understates damage when defects co-occur. Real translations carry multiple defects, so linear composition would be optimistic/lenient.
  • Compounding mechanism. When meaning is mistranslated and content is omitted, the passage loses the redundancy respondents lean on; damage compounds. Even MCQ — robust to single defects because options allow reconstruction/guessing — collapses toward chance (0.43–0.50) once two defects stack.
  • MCQ's fluency-blindness is bounded. The earlier "MCQ misses single defects" limitation does not extend to realistic multi-defect translations, which MCQ clearly catches. Good for field QC.
  • Fluency defects aren't inert in context. grammar 10% (flat on its own) still amplifies omission (I = +0.16). Fluency damage reduces the slack that helps respondents absorb adequacy damage.
  • Resolves the "additivity UNTESTED" status. Step-2/step2b left additivity untestable via MQM. This causal manipulation tests it directly (no MQM involved) and rejects it — strengthening the empirical-benchmarking deployment path (calibrate on real multi-defect translations) over the compositional model. If composition is kept, it needs a superadditive/interaction term.
