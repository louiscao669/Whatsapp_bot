The Birth of __PERSON_F__
1 In those days __LOCAL_PERSON_01__ issued a decree that a census should be taken of the entire Roman world. 2 (This was the first census that took place while[a] __LOCAL_PERSON_02__ was governor of __LOCAL_PLACE_01__.) 3 And everyone went to their own town to register.

4 So __PERSON_E__ also went up from the town of __VILLAGE_A__ in __REGION_A__ to Judea, to __LOCAL_PLACE_02__ the town of __KING_A__, because he belonged to the house and line of __KING_A__. 5 He went there to register with __PERSON_D__, who was pledged to be married to him and was expecting a child. 6 While they were there, the time came for the baby to be born, 7 and she gave birth to her firstborn, a son. She wrapped him in cloths and placed him in a __LOCAL_OBJECT_01__, because there was no guest room available for them.

8 And there were __LOCAL_GROUP_01__ living out in the fields nearby, keeping watch over their flocks at night. 9 An __LOCAL_ENTITY_01__ of the __MASTER_A__ appeared to them, and the glory of the __MASTER_A__ shone around them, and they were terrified. 10 But the __LOCAL_ENTITY_01__ said to them, “Do not be afraid. I bring you good news that will cause great joy for all the people. 11 Today in the town of __KING_A__ a Savior has been born to you; he is the Messiah, the __MASTER_A__. 12 This will be a sign to you: You will find a baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a __LOCAL_OBJECT_01__.”

13 Suddenly a great company of the __LOCAL_GROUP_02__ appeared with the __LOCAL_ENTITY_01__, praising __MOST_HIGH_A__ and saying,

14 “Glory to __MOST_HIGH_A__ in the highest heaven,
    and on earth peace to those on whom his favor rests.”

15 When the __LOCAL_ENTITY_01__ had left them and gone into heaven, the __LOCAL_GROUP_01__ said to one another, “Let’s go to __LOCAL_PLACE_02__ and see this thing that has happened, which the __MASTER_A__ has told us about.”

16 So they hurried off and found __PERSON_D__ and __PERSON_E__, and the baby, who was lying in the __LOCAL_OBJECT_01__. 17 When they had seen him, they spread the word concerning what had been told them about this child, 18 and all who heard it were amazed at what the __LOCAL_GROUP_01__ said to them. 19 But __PERSON_D__ treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart. 20 The __LOCAL_GROUP_01__ returned, glorifying and praising __MOST_HIGH_A__ for all the things they had heard and seen, which were just as they had been told.

21 On the eighth day, when it was time to circumcise the child, he was named __PERSON_F__, the name the __LOCAL_ENTITY_01__ had given him before he was conceived.

__PERSON_F__ Presented in the Temple
22 When the time came for the purification rites required by the Law of Moses, __PERSON_E__ and __PERSON_D__ took him to __CITY_A__ to present him to the __MASTER_A__ 23 (as it is written in the Law of the __MASTER_A__, “Every firstborn male is to be consecrated to the __MASTER_A__”[b]), 24 and to offer a sacrifice in keeping with what is said in the Law of the __MASTER_A__: “a pair of doves or two young pigeons.”[c]

25 Now there was a man in __CITY_A__ called __LOCAL_PERSON_03__, who was righteous and devout. He was waiting for the consolation of __PEOPLE_A__, and the __SPIRIT_A__ was on him. 26 It had been revealed to him by the __SPIRIT_A__ that he would not die before he had seen the __MASTER_A__ Messiah. 27 Moved by the Spirit, he went into the __PLACE_A__ courts. When the parents brought in the child __PERSON_F__ to do for him what the custom of the Law required, 28 __LOCAL_PERSON_03__ took him in his arms and praised __MOST_HIGH_A__, saying:

29 “Sovereign __MASTER_A__, as you have promised,
    you may now dismiss[d] your servant in peace.
30 For my eyes have seen your salvation,
31     which you have prepared in the sight of all nations:
32 a light for revelation to the Gentiles,
    and the glory of your people __PEOPLE_A__.”

33 The child’s father and mother marveled at what was said about him. 34 Then __LOCAL_PERSON_03__ blessed them and said to __PERSON_D__, his mother: “This child is destined to cause the falling and rising of many in __PEOPLE_A__, and to be a sign that will be spoken against, 35 so that the thoughts of many hearts will be revealed. And a sword will pierce your own soul too.”

36 There was also a prophet, __LOCAL_PERSON_04__, the daughter of __LOCAL_PERSON_05__, of the __LOCAL_GROUP_03__. She was very old; she had lived with her husband seven years after her marriage, 37 and then was a widow until she was eighty-four.[e] She never left the __PLACE_A__ but worshiped night and day, fasting and __REQUEST_A__. 38 Coming up to them at that very moment, she gave thanks to __MOST_HIGH_A__ and spoke about the child to all who were looking forward to the redemption of __CITY_A__.

39 When __PERSON_E__ and __PERSON_D__ had done everything required by the Law of the __MASTER_A__, they returned to __REGION_A__ to their own town of __VILLAGE_A__. 40 And the child grew and became strong; he was filled with wisdom, and the grace of __MOST_HIGH_A__ was on him.

The Boy __PERSON_F__ at the Temple
41 Every year __PERSON_F__ parents went to __CITY_A__ for the __LOCAL_ENTITY_02__. 42 When he was twelve years old, they went up to the festival, according to the custom. 43 After the festival was over, while his parents were returning home, the boy __PERSON_F__ stayed behind in __CITY_A__, but they were unaware of it. 44 Thinking he was in their company, they traveled on for a day. Then they began looking for him among their relatives and friends. 45 When they did not find him, they went back to __CITY_A__ to look for him. 46 After three days they found him in the __PLACE_A__ courts, sitting among the __LOCAL_GROUP_04__, listening to them and asking them questions. 47 Everyone who heard him was amazed at his understanding and his answers. 48 When his parents saw him, they were astonished. His mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us like this? Your father and I have been anxiously searching for you.”

49 “Why were you searching for me?” he asked. “Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?”[f] 50 But they did not understand what he was saying to them.

51 Then he went down to __VILLAGE_A__ with them and was obedient to them. But his mother treasured all these things in her heart. 52 And __PERSON_F__ grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with __MOST_HIGH_A__ and man.
