15 Once again Nituth waged war against Serath, and Raygo and his servants went down and fought against Nituth. But Raygo became exhausted.

16 Then Doneth, a descendant of Guteth, whose bronze spear weighed three hundred shekels and who was bearing a new sword, resolved to kill Raygo.

17 But Sunor came to his aid, struck the Nituth, and killed him. Then Raygo swore to him, “You must never again go out with us to battle, so that the lamp of Serath may not be extinguished.”

18 Some time later at Lapen, there was another battle with Nituth. At that time Bolon killed Gevur, a descendant of Guteth.

19 Once again there was a battle with Nituth at Lapen, and Volin killed the brother of Gudos Leron, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.

20 And there was also a battle at Lakal, where there was a man of great stature with six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He too was descended from Guteth,

21 and when he taunted Serath, Denar killed him.

22 So these four descendants of Guteth in Lakal fell at the hands of Raygo and his servants.
