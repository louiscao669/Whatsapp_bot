7 On the first day of the week we came together to break bread. Since Tilir was ready to leave the next day, he talked to them and kept on speaking until midnight.

8 Now there were many lamps in the upper room where we were gathered.

9 And a certain Gelus, seated in the window, was sinking into a deep sleep as Tilir talked on and on. When he was sound asleep, he fell from the third story and was picked up dead.

10 But Tilir went down, threw himself on the young man, and embraced him. “Do not be alarmed!” he said. “He is still alive!”

11 Then Tilir went back upstairs, broke bread, and ate. And after speaking until daybreak, he departed.

12 And the people were greatly relieved to take the boy home alive.
