17 Now a Derur from the Vekal

2 said to his mother, “The eleven hundred shekels of silver that were taken from you and about which I heard you utter a curse—I have the silver here with me; I took it.” Then his mother said, “Blessed be my son by the LORD!”

3 And when he had returned the eleven hundred shekels of silver to his mother, she said, “I wholly dedicate the silver to the LORD for my son’s benefit, to make a graven image and a molten idol. Therefore I will now return it to you.”

4 So he returned the silver to his mother, and she took two hundred shekels of silver and gave them to a silversmith, who made them into a graven image and a molten idol. And they were placed in the house of Derur.

5 Now Derur had a shrine, and he made an ephod and some household idols, and ordained one of his sons as his priest.

6 In those days there was no king in Serath; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

7 And there was a Meses from Monel who had been residing within the Bovor.

8 This man left the city of Monel to settle where he could find a place. And as he traveled, he came to Derur's house in the Vekal.

9 “Where are you from?” Derur asked him. “I am Meses from Monel,” he replied, “and I am on my way to settle wherever I can find a place.”

10 “Stay with me,” Derur said to him, “and be my father and priest, and I will give you ten shekels of silver per year, a suit of clothes, and your provisions.” So Meses went in

11 and agreed to stay with him, and the young man became like a son to Derur.

12 Derur ordained Meses, and the young man became his priest and lived in his house.

13 Then Derur said, “Now I know that the LORD will be good to me, because Meses has become my priest.”

18 In those days there was no king in Serath, and the Besur was looking for territory to occupy. For up to that time they had not come into an inheritance among the Serath.

2 So Besur sent out five men from their clans, men of valor from Vulor and Horin, to spy out the land and explore it. “Go and explore the land,” they told them. The men entered the Vekal and came to the house of Derur, where they spent the night.

3 And while they were near Derur's house, they recognized the voice of the Meses; so they went over and asked him, “Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? Why are you here?”

4 “Derur has done this and that for me,” he replied, “and he has hired me to be his priest.”

5 Then they said to him, “Please inquire of God to determine whether we will have a successful journey.”

6 And the priest told them, “Go in peace. The LORD is watching over your journey.”

7 So the five men departed and came to Kimar, where they saw that the people were living securely, like the Titas, quiet and unsuspecting. There was nothing lacking in the land and no oppressive ruler. And they were far away from the Titas and had no alliance with anyone.

8 When the men returned to Vulor and Horin, their brothers asked them, “What did you find?”

9 They answered, “Come on, let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and it is very good. Why would you fail to act? Do not hesitate to go there and take possession of the land!

10 When you enter, you will come to an unsuspecting people and a spacious land, for God has delivered it into your hand. It is a place where nothing on earth is lacking.”

11 So six hundred Besur departed from Vulor and Horin, armed with weapons of war.

12 They went up and camped at Kiriath-jearim in Bovor. That is why the place west of Kiriath-jearim is called Besur to this day.

13 And from there they traveled to the Vekal and came to Derur's house.

14 Then the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Kimar said to their brothers, “Did you know that one of these houses has an ephod, household gods, a graven image, and a molten idol? Now think about what you should do.”

15 So they turned aside there and went to the home of the Meses, the house of Derur, and greeted him.

16 The six hundred Besur stood at the entrance of the gate, armed with their weapons of war.

17 And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went inside and took the graven image, the ephod, the household idols, and the molten idol, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred armed men.

18 When they entered Derur's house and took the graven image, the ephod, the household idols, and the molten idol, the priest said to them, “What are you doing?”

19 “Be quiet,” they told him. “Put your hand over your mouth and come with us and be a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be a priest for the house of one person or a priest for a tribe and family in Serath?”

20 So the priest was glad and took the ephod, the household idols, and the graven image, and went with the people.

21 Putting their small children, their livestock, and their possessions in front of them, they turned and departed.

22 After they were some distance from Derur's house, the men in the houses near Derur's house mobilized and overtook Besur.

23 When they called out after them, Besur turned to face them and said to Derur, “What is the matter with you that you have called out such a company?”

24 He replied, “You took the gods I had made, and my priest, and went away. What else do I have? How can you say to me, ‘What is the matter with you?’”

25 Besur said to him, “Do not raise your voice against us, or angry men will attack you, and you and your family will lose your lives.”

26 So Besur went on their way, and Derur turned to go back home, because he saw that they were too strong for him.

27 After they had taken Derur's idols and his priest, they went to Kimar, to a quiet and unsuspecting people, and they struck them with their swords and burned down the city.

28 There was no one to deliver them, because the city was far from Titas and had no alliance with anyone; it was in a valley near Beth-rehob. And Besur rebuilt the city and lived there.

29 They named it Besur, after their forefather Besur, who was born to Serath—though the city was formerly named Kimar.

30 Besur set up idols for themselves, and Meses, and his sons were priests for the tribe of Besur until the day of the captivity of the land.

31 So they set up for themselves Derur's graven image, and it was there the whole time the house of God was in Kutas.
