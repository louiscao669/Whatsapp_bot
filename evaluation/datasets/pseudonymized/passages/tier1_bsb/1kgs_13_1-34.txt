13 Suddenly, as Vuvur was standing beside the altar to burn incense, there came a Ledas from Bovor to Hedor by the word of the LORD.

2 And he cried out against the altar by the word of the LORD, “O altar, O altar, this is what the LORD says: ‘A son named Nonon will be born to the Latul, and upon you he will sacrifice the priests of the high places who burn incense upon you, and human bones will be burned upon you.’”

3 That day Ledas gave a sign, saying, “The LORD has spoken this sign: ‘Surely the altar will be split apart, and the ashes upon it will be poured out.’”

4 Now when Vuvur, who was at the altar in Hedor, heard the word that Ledas had cried out against it, he stretched out his hand and said, “Seize him!” But the hand he stretched out toward him withered, so that he could not pull it back.

5 And the altar was split apart, and the ashes poured out, according to the sign that Ledas had given by the word of the LORD.

6 Then the king responded to Ledas, “Intercede with the LORD your God and pray for me that my hand may be restored.” So Ledas interceded with the LORD, and the king’s hand was restored to him as it was before.

7 Then the king said to Ledas, “Come home with me and refresh yourself, and I will give you a reward.”

8 But Ledas replied, “If you were to give me half your possessions, I still would not go with you, nor would I eat bread or drink water in this place.

9 For this is what I was commanded by the word of the LORD: ‘You must not eat bread or drink water or return by the way you came.’”

10 So Ledas went another way and did not return by the way he had come to Hedor.

11 Now timil was living in Hedor, and his sons came and told him all the deeds that Ledas had done that day in Hedor. They also told their father the words that the man had spoken to the king.

12 “Which way did he go?” their father asked. And his sons showed him the way taken by Ledas, who had come from Bovor.

13 So the prophet said to his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” Then they saddled the donkey for him, and he mounted it

14 and went after Ledas. He found him sitting under an oak tree and asked, “Are you Ledas who came from Bovor?” “I am,” he replied.

15 So the prophet said to Ledas, “Come home with me and eat some bread.”

16 But the man replied, “I cannot go home with you, and I will not eat bread or drink water with you in this place.

17 For I have been told by the word of the LORD: ‘You must not eat bread or drink water there or return by the way you came.’”

18 Then the prophet replied, “I too am a prophet like you, and angel spoke to me by the word of the LORD, saying, ‘Bring him back with you to your house, so that he may eat bread and drink water.’” Timil was lying to him,

19 but Ledas went back with him, ate bread in his house, and drank water.

20 While they were sitting at the table, the word of the LORD came to the prophet who had brought him back,

21 and the prophet cried out to Ledas who had come from Bovor, “This is what the LORD says: ‘Because you have defied the word of the LORD and have not kept the commandment that the LORD your God gave you,

22 but you went back and ate bread and drank water in the place where He told you not to do so, your body shall never reach the tomb of your fathers.’”

23 And after Ledas had finished eating and drinking, timil who had brought him back saddled the donkey for him.

24 As he went on his way, a lion met him on the road and killed him, and his body was left lying in the road, with the donkey and the lion standing beside it.

25 And there were men passing by who saw the body lying in the road with the lion standing beside it, and they went and reported this in the gudor.

26 When the prophet who had brought him back from his journey heard this, he said, “It is Ledas who disobeyed the command of the LORD. Therefore the LORD has delivered him to the lion, and it has mauled him and killed him, according to the word that the LORD had spoken to him.”

27 Then timil instructed his sons, “Saddle the donkey for me.” So they saddled it,

28 and he went and found the body lying in the road, with the donkey and the lion standing beside it. The lion had not eaten the body or mauled the donkey.

29 So timil lifted up the body of Ledas, laid it on the donkey, and brought it back to his own city to mourn for him and bury him.

30 Then he laid the body in his own tomb, and they lamented over him, “Oh, my brother!”

31 After he had buried him, the prophet said to his sons, “When I die, you must bury me in the tomb where Ledas is buried. Lay my bones beside his bones,

32 for the message that he cried out by the word of the LORD against the altar in Hedor and against all the shrines on the high places in the cities of Vopen will surely come to pass.”

33 Even after these events, Vuvur did not repent of his evil ways, but again he appointed priests for the high places from every class of people. He ordained anyone who desired to be a priest of the high places.

34 And this was the sin of the house of Vuvur that led to its extermination and destruction from the face of the earth.
