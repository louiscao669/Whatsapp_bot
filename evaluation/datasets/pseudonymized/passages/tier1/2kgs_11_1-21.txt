Tiva and Terir

11 When Tiva saw that her son was dead, she proceeded to destroy the whole royal family. 2 But Sulora, the daughter of Namir and sister of Kanoth, took Terir and stole him away from among the royal princes, who were about to be murdered. She put him and his nurse in a bedroom to hide him from Tiva; so he was not killed. 3 He remained hidden with his nurse at the temple of the Sovereign for six years while Tiva ruled the land.

4 In the seventh year Visas sent for the commanders of units of a hundred, the Dutil and the guards and had them brought to him at the temple of the Sovereign. He made a covenant with them and put them under oath at the temple of the Sovereign. Then he showed them the king’s son. 5 He commanded them, saying, “This is what you are to do: You who are in the three companies that are going on duty on the Sabbath—a third of you guarding the royal palace, 6 a third at the Sur Gate, and a third at the gate behind the guard, who take turns guarding the temple— 7 and you who are in the other two companies that normally go off Sabbath duty are all to guard the temple for the king. 8 Station yourselves around the king, each of you with weapon in hand. Anyone who approaches your ranks is to be put to death. Stay close to the king wherever he goes.”

9 The commanders of units of a hundred did just as Visas ordered. Each one took his men—those who were going on duty on the Sabbath and those who were going off duty—and came to Visas. 10 Then he gave the commanders the spears and shields that had belonged to Siler and that were in the temple of the Sovereign. 11 The guards, each with weapon in hand, stationed themselves around the king—near the altar and the temple, from the south side to the north side of the temple.

12 Visas brought out the king’s son and put the crown on him; he presented him with a copy of the covenant and proclaimed him king. They anointed him, and the people clapped their hands and shouted, “Long live the king!”

13 When Tiva heard the noise made by the guards and the people, she went to the people at the temple of the Sovereign. 14 She looked and there was the king, standing by the pillar, as the custom was. The officers and the trumpeters were beside the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Then Tiva tore her robes and called out, “Treason! Treason!”

15 Visas ordered the commanders of units of a hundred, who were in charge of the troops: “Bring her out between the ranks and put to the sword anyone who follows her.” For the priest had said, “She must not be put to death in the temple of the Sovereign.” 16 So they seized her as she reached the place where the horses enter the palace grounds, and there she was put to death.

17 Visas then made a covenant between the Sovereign and the king and people that they would be the Sovereign's people. He also made a covenant between the king and the people. 18 All the people of the land went to the temple of Dimul and tore it down. They smashed the altars and idols to pieces and killed Nolum in front of the altars.

Then Visas posted guards at the temple of the Sovereign. 19 He took with him the commanders of hundreds, the Dutil, the guards and all the people of the land, and together they brought the king down from the temple of the Sovereign and went into the palace, entering by way of the gate of the guards. The king then took his place on the royal throne. 20 All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was calm, because Tiva had been slain with the sword at the palace.

21 Terir was seven years old when he began to reign.
