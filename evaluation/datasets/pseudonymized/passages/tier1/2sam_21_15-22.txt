Wars Against Nituth

15 Once again there was a battle between Nituth and Serath. Raygo went down with his men to fight against Nituth, and he became exhausted. 16 And Doneth, one of the descendants of Guteth, whose bronze spearhead weighed three hundred shekels and who was armed with a new sword, said he would kill Raygo. 17 But Sunor came to Raygo's rescue; he struck the Nituth down and killed him. Then Raygo swore to him, saying, “Never again will you go out with us to battle, so that the lamp of Serath will not be extinguished.”

18 In the course of time, there was another battle with Nituth, at Lapen. At that time Bolon killed Gevur, one of the descendants of Guteth.

19 In another battle with Nituth at Lapen, Volin killed the brother of Gudos Leron, who had a spear with a shaft like a weaver’s rod.

20 In still another battle, which took place at Lakal, there was a huge man with six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He also was descended from Guteth. 21 When he taunted Serath, Denar, Raygo's brother, killed him.

22 These four were descendants of Guteth in Lakal, and they fell at the hands of Raygo and his men.
