# Final 77 compared with the original Hard66

**63 of the original 66 questions are in the final bank (95.5%).** Of those, 53 have identical question, key, competitor and verse window; 10 have revisions. The final bank adds 14 candidates from the original 85-item pool and drops 3 Hard66 items. The net gain is 11.

An item is identified by its source passage and source content ID. I verified the archived English selection against all 66 IDs, questions, keys and windows in the published English render source. The comparison uses the original English open-response wording.
