What the test establishes

The combined prompt, structured evidence and gate changes enforce the intended conservative boundary on these cases. No live response was rejected solely by the extra code gate after all five original criteria passed; this run does not isolate the independent benefit of the gate.

The next refinement would distinguish categorical changes in certainty from vague degrees, and allow clearly different vague-to-exact frequencies without demanding exact values on both sides. That refinement has not been made during this frozen test.

Expected labels were authored by the assistant under a deliberately strict experimental policy and are not independently human-validated. Some vague contrasts may be linguistically plausible mistranslations despite being rejected by this policy. The scores measure reference-label agreement.
