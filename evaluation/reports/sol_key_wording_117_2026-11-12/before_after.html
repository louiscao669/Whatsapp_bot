Correct-answer wording in the Sol pilot

117 published questions screened. 16 rewrites passed full review, 4 were held, and 97 kept their original wording. The live bank still serves its published release.

The reviewer checked each key for avoidable restatement of the question. 13 keys also had at least three more characters than every distractor and at least 35% more than the middle distractor. Counts exclude punctuation, whitespace and option E. The reviewer checked any extra length against the requested fact. When all four choices shared the same flagged introduction, the pipeline removed that opening from the other answers and rechecked the full question.

Choosing the longest of A–D, with random selection among ties, would score 31.1% on the published wording and 22.5% if only the passing revisions were adopted. Choosing the shortest would move from 29.3% to 33.8%.
