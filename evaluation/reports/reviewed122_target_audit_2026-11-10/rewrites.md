## 1 Kings 13:26–28 · Prepared QA

Source ID `t1_1kgs13:cj4m`

**Original**　老先知吩咐儿子们做什么？接着他自己又做了什么？

**Revised**　老先知叫他的儿子们为他做什么？

What did the old prophet tell his sons to do for him?

| Choice | Chinese | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A (key)** | 为他备驴。 | Get his donkey ready for him to ride. |
| B | 为他把狮子赶离路。 | Drive the lion away from the road for him. |
| C | 为他取那神人的驴。 | Fetch the man of God's donkey for him. |
| D | 为他把死人的身体带回来。 | Bring the dead man's body back to him. |

**What the rewrite kept.** Kept only the instruction to his sons to saddle the donkey. Dropped his subsequent discovery of the body and the observations about the lion, leaving one independently gradable fact.

## Judges 18:27–28 · Generated from scratch

Source ID `judg_18_1-31/v27-28:1`

**Original**　哪两种情况使来士人得不到援救？

**Revised**　拉亿人没有被任何人拯救的地理原因是什么？

What geographical circumstance left the people of Laish without anyone to rescue them?

| Choice | Chinese | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A (key)** | 他们离西顿很远。 | They lived a long way from Sidon. |
| B | 他们被一条宽广的河流与西顿隔开。 | They were separated from Sidon by a wide river. |
| C | 他们住在一座偏远的山上，四周被悬崖包围。 | They lived on a remote hill surrounded by cliffs. |
| D | 他们离伯利合很远。 | They lived a long way from Beth Rehob. |

**What the rewrite kept.** Kept the distance from Sidon as a cause of Laish having no rescuer. Dropped the separate fact that they had no ties with anyone else; specifying a geographical circumstance makes the question single-target.
