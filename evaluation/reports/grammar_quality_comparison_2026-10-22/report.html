Why Sol is a better syntax condition

The original model-run input records 37 classifier operations, 37 aspect operations, 20 function-word deletions, 33 local reorderings and 3 connective changes. That is 94 of 130 operations in the first three categories (72.3%). Its reorderings also sometimes split words. Sol preserves all characters in every edited clause and usually makes an identifiable local construction error. Character preservation alone does not prove that every word or meaning is preserved; the displayed examples and the full comparison support the qualitative assessment.

What still limits the new version

15 of 49 Sol edits (30.6%) can be reproduced by moving one existing function character, such as 的, 了, 就, 在 or 呢. That percentage is an edit-count statistic, separate from the passage coverage. These shifts can break syntax, but they do not all meet a preference for larger phrase movement. Larger examples include moving 在米迦那里 inside 他的儿子 and moving 从他们的宗族中 inside 五个勇士.

Recommended next comparison

Use Sol as the structural-defect candidate. To assess effectiveness, compare clean, original and Sol passages with the same answering model, questions, option order and verse windows. Match the selected clauses or at least local evidence exposure before attributing any accuracy difference to structural severity. Separate particle-only rearrangements from larger phrase changes, and manually verify a sample of both approvals and rejections. Do not interpret a greater accuracy drop caused by damaged words or altered facts as stronger grammar sensitivity.
