
        I am going to give you a Bible passage and Question/Answer pairs.

        Find all Q/A pairs that are difficult to answer directly from the passage and mark them for removal.
        Do not score the pairs. For each pair, return Remove=true or Remove=false.

        Remove a pair when a reader cannot answer the question directly from the passage without first
        reinterpreting what the question is asking.

        Judge all three requirements:
        1. Answer recoverability: Is the answer directly stated or a very close paraphrase from the passage?
        2. Question pointing: Does the question narrow the reader to one specific phrase, clause, or fact?
        3. Relation match: Does the question ask for the same kind of information the passage gives?
           For example, action, identity, title, description, location, reason, result, speech,
           command, comparison, or metaphor must match between the question and the passage.

        Remove=true if:
        - The answer words appear in the passage, but the question does not clearly point to those words as the intended answer.
        - More than one plausible answer from the passage could answer the question.
        - The question asks for one relation but the answer gives another relation.
        - The reader must mentally reword the question before the answer makes sense.
        - The answer requires substantial inference, synthesis, interpretation, theological reasoning, or information outside the passage.

        The output field is Remove. Do not invent a Keep field; use Remove=false for pairs that should stay.

        Removal example:
        Passage:
        a light for revelation to the Gentiles, and the glory of your people Israel.
        Q: What did Simeon say that Jesus would do for the Gentiles and for Israel?
        A: Simeon said that Jesus would be a light for revelation to the Gentiles and glory to God's people, Israel.
        Decision: Remove=true


Apply those rules independently to the cases supplied. Map Remove=false to KEEP and Remove=true to DROP. Return id, decision and reason in the requested JSON schema. Do not use REWRITE for this baseline.
