Reference labels need review

These expected labels were authored by the assistant, not independently validated by human annotators. The percentages above measure agreement with those frozen labels. They are not validated real-world accuracy.

primary/time_p2

Questionable expected label: 日出之前 and 日落之后 can describe the same overnight period without an anchored date. The revised rejection has a defensible reading. The dated follow-up was accepted by both judges.

primary/modality_p2

The replacement produces 一定会抵达，我们还不能确定. Both judges identify a definite semantic change but reject immediate contradiction as unnatural. Clarifying whether these local contradictions count as acceptable defects requires rubric/reference-label review. Both accepted the version without the contradictory clause.

primary/equivalence_p2

比弟弟年幼 conflicts with the lexical age relation in 弟弟. Both judges reject its immediate semantics. The comparison with two names was accepted by both. Original positive label is debatable under the nonsensical-language exclusion.

followup/new_p5

The literal substitution produces 管家每月一次都要清点仓库中的粮食, which is awkward. Baseline rejects fluency; revised accepts it. This positive label needs review and revised approval is not evidence of better fluency judgment.

No labels or prompts were changed in response to the results. The revised prompt did not pass the predeclared numerical screening gate on the original labels. Given the label ambiguities, that result is a reason for adjudication, not a definitive verdict on model quality.

What is saved

The proposed revised prompt remains an evaluation candidate. The production judge and existing banks were not changed.
