## User Study of Translation Quality of AI Systems

Toby Jiajun Li

### ABOUT THIS RESEARCH

You are being asked to participate in a research study. Scientists do research to answer questions and learn new information. Some research might help change or improve the way we do things in the future. This consent information will tell you more about the study to help you decide whether you want to participate. Please read this information before agreeing to be in the study.

### TAKING PART IN THIS STUDY IS VOLUNTARY

You may choose not to take part in the study or may choose to leave the study at any time. Deciding not to participate, or deciding to leave the study later, will not result in any penalty or affect your relationship with the student researcher or with the University of Notre Dame.

As an alternative to participating in the study, you may choose not to take part.

### WHY IS THIS STUDY BEING DONE?

The purpose of this study is to test a new way of checking whether a translation is good, for languages where no expert is available to check it. We are testing the translation, not you.

You were selected as a possible participant because you speak the target language, you are 18 or older, and you received a written invitation from the student researcher or from someone who forwarded it to you.

The study is being conducted by Toby Jiajun Li, Department of Computer Science and Engineering at University of Notre Dame, and Louis Cao, University of Notre Dame. It is funded by Every Tribe Every Nation.

### HOW MANY PEOPLE WILL TAKE PART?

If you agree to participate, you will be one of approximately 12 to 20 participants taking part in this study.

### WHAT WILL HAPPEN DURING THE STUDY?

If you agree to be in the study, you will be asked to do the following things:

1. Background questionnaire (approximately 3–10 minutes). Confirms eligibility, then collects demographic information and questions about your background and familiarity with the source material.

2. Reading and question sessions. Each session presents passages in a target language followed by questions asking only what the passage says, answered as multiple choice or as a short, typed response. Passages vary systematically across sessions — some unmodified, some with a designed proportion of content removed or replaced, some with grammatical errors only, and some produced by literal machine translation — and each participant encounters a range of these conditions in a counterbalanced order. Participants set their own pace. Answering all questions takes approximately 1 hour and up to 2 hours.

All of this happens remotely on your own phone or computer, through a study website. There are no appointments and no deadlines. No photographs, audio recordings, or video recordings are made at any point.

**IMPORTANT** — something you should know before you agree: some of the passages you will read have been deliberately changed to contain errors. Words may be missing, replaced with the wrong word, or the grammar may be wrong. We will not tell you which passages these are while the study is running, because knowing would change how you read them, and that is exactly what we are measuring. This means some passages will be hard or confusing to read. That is expected and it is not a problem with you. There is no penalty for a wrong answer and your payment does not depend on answering correctly. When you finish, we will explain which passages were altered and how.

### WHAT ARE THE RISKS OF TAKING PART IN THE STUDY?

This study presents no more than minimal risk and involves no physical procedures, substances, or in-person contact.

The principal risk is the loss of confidentiality common to any online study, since the dataset associates an account identifier with participants' responses; this is mitigated by storing identifiers as a keyed hash held separately from responses and destroying the link once data collection ends.

### WHAT ARE THE POTENTIAL BENEFITS OF TAKING PART IN THE STUDY?

We don’t expect you to receive any benefit from taking part in this study, but we hope to learn things that will help scientists in the future.

### HOW WILL MY INFORMATION BE PROTECTED?

Efforts will be made to keep your personal information confidential, though we cannot guarantee this absolutely, and your information may be disclosed if required by law. No information which could identify you will be shared in publications about this study or in databases where results may be stored; details such as age, education, and country will be removed or grouped together so that individuals cannot be recognized. No audio or video recordings are made in this study.

Your typed answers are sent, with no identifier attached, to an automated scoring service. That service does not use this text to train its systems. It may keep the text briefly to check for misuse of its service.

Organizations that may inspect and/or copy your research records for quality assurance and data analysis include groups such as the study investigator and his research associates, the University of Notre Dame Institutional Review Board or its designees, and (as allowed by law) state or federal agencies, especially the Office for Human Research Protections (OHRP), who may need to access the research records.

### WILL MY INFORMATION BE USED FOR RESEARCH IN THE FUTURE?

Information collected from you for this study may be used for future research studies or shared with other researchers for future research. If this happens, information which could identify you will be removed before any information is shared. Since identifying information will be removed, we will not ask for your additional consent.

### WILL I BE PAID FOR PARTICIPATION?

You will receive up to US$20 in total for taking part, sent as a digital gift card. You do not have to finish the study to be paid. Payment does not depend on your answers being correct — only on answering.
