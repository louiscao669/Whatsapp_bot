How the sizes were assigned

The original GPT-4.1-mini judge provided an MQM category, severity, source and target spans, and an explanation. The size labels here come from a separate bilingual review of the saved text. I checked the flagged claim and consulted surrounding verses for apparent gaps. Each flag counts once, unweighted. An output can have errors beyond the one the judge identified.

  • Word. A single missing English lexical item, such as a material, qualifier, or kinship term. An article attached to a term does not increase its size.
  • Phrase. A missing group of words without its own predication, such as a beneficiary, time expression, or prepositional phrase. Successive noun phrases in a genealogy keep this label.
  • Clause or larger. A whole assertion, action, or predication disappears, including a reduced relative clause such as “left there by the fishermen.”

I assigned the largest independently missing unit within the reported issue where a flag covered several. I counted a separable loss alongside other damage when the retained Chinese made the missing unit identifiable. I left ambiguous substitutions, garbled output, and naturally implicit Chinese expressions outside the size counts. The review did not examine unflagged output for missed omissions. An independent bilingual annotator has yet to validate these retrospective labels.
